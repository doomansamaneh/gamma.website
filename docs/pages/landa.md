# Build Landa Product Page for Doman Samaneh

## Objective

Create the official **Landa product page** inside the Doman Samaneh corporate Astro website.

Landa is one of the four products of:

**دومان سامانه**

The page must introduce Landa as a real product in the Doman Samaneh product ecosystem and provide a clear path to the dedicated Landa website:

**https://landa-sme.ir/**

Do NOT copy the Landa website page-for-page.

This is a **corporate product presentation page** on Doman Samaneh, not a replacement for the Landa website.

---

# 1. Source of Truth

Before implementing the page, inspect:

**https://landa-sme.ir/**

Use the existing Landa website as the primary source for:

- product positioning
- capabilities
- terminology
- features
- target customers
- pricing information if needed
- product screenshots/images where legally/technically available
- FAQ topics
- product identity

Do not invent features.

If information is unclear, prefer the existing Landa website or existing project content.

Do not copy obsolete or irrelevant website content.

---

# 2. Product Positioning

Landa is:

**نرم افزار حسابداری آنلاین و ابری برای کسب و کارهای کوچک و متوسط**

Core positioning:

- Online accounting
- Cloud accounting
- Financial management
- Simple and fast
- Suitable for small and medium-sized businesses
- Accessible through browser
- No local software installation
- Works across devices

The product should be presented as a serious business application, not merely a simple bookkeeping tool.

# 30. Final Acceptance Criteria

The final page should answer these questions immediately:

### What is Landa?

A cloud-based online accounting and financial management solution.

### Who is it for?

Small and medium-sized businesses.

### What does it do?

Accounting, sales, purchases, receipts/payments, expenses, reporting, payroll, electronic invoicing and related capabilities supported by the product.

### Why should I care?

It provides a simpler, online and accessible way to manage accounting and financial operations.

### Where can I learn more or use it?

**https://landa-sme.ir/**

The final visual result must feel like a **real enterprise product page inside the Doman Samaneh ecosystem**, not a generic SaaS landing page.

Before finishing, inspect the page on desktop and mobile and fix:

- excessive whitespace
- inconsistent spacing
- weak hierarchy
- unnecessary cards
- awkward Persian wrapping
- oversized headings
- poor screenshot presentation
- inconsistent CTA styles
- inconsistent logo sizing
- responsive issues

Do not modify unrelated pages unless a shared component must be changed to maintain consistency.
